Privacy policy

For installhub.org, SafeInstall and TechMate · Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no tracking cookies, no advertising profiles. SafeInstall counts visits anonymously, without cookies.
  • What you type into SafeInstall stays on your phone. If you tap Send, it passes through our email service to the addresses you choose, and we don't keep a copy.
  • Questions to Ask TechMate AI go to our AI provider so it can answer them. We don't keep the conversation.
  • We never sell your information, and sponsors never get it.

Who we are

InstallHub is a small Australian business, based in Victoria, that runs installhub.org, SafeInstall (safeinstall.org) and TechMate (techmate.installhub.org). Small businesses aren't always covered by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), but we choose to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles anyway. This policy explains how.

What we collect, and why

Every site: standard web logs

Our web host, Netlify, automatically records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time. It's used to deliver the sites, keep them secure and fix problems. We don't use it to identify or profile you.

SafeInstall

  • Saved on your device, not by us. Your name, business details, phone, licence and ticket numbers and expiry dates, worker names, chosen state, and the fact you've accepted the terms are saved in your browser's local storage, so you don't have to retype them. We can't see this. Tap "Clear my saved details" in SafeInstall, or clear your browser data, to remove it.
  • Not saved at all. Job answers, signatures and photos aren't kept once you start a new job.
  • When you tap Send. The PDF, the summary and the email addresses you enter are sent to our email function and passed to our email delivery provider, Resend, which delivers them. We don't keep a copy or use them for anything else. Resend keeps delivery records, and may keep message details for a limited time, under its own privacy policy. Error logs on our host may briefly record that a send failed, but not the document.
  • Anonymous visit counts. SafeInstall uses GoatCounter to count page visits and a few actions: pre-start sent, SWMS sent, PDF saved and app installed. GoatCounter uses no cookies and doesn't store your IP address. It sees the page and action name, your browser and device type, screen size, rough location (country) and the site you came from. Nothing you type into SafeInstall is sent to it. We use the counts only to see whether SafeInstall is being used and what to improve.
  • Other people's details. A SWMS or pre-start can include other people's names (such as workers) and email addresses. Only include them where they know about it and you're entitled to share them.

TechMate

  • Remembered on your device. TechMate may remember the panel you last looked at, in your browser's local storage, to save you time. We can't see it.
  • Ask TechMate AI. When you ask a question, the conversation is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce an answer. If the guides don't cover the question, the AI may also run a web search. We don't store the conversation. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and may keep it for a limited time, for example to detect misuse. Your IP address is used briefly, in memory only, to limit how many questions can be asked per hour. We record daily cost totals, not questions. Please don't type customer names, addresses, alarm codes or passwords.
  • Suggest and contact form. We receive what you type and, if you choose to give it, your email address. Submissions are stored by Netlify Forms. We use them to fix or improve the sites and, if you asked, to reply. No mailing lists.

InstallHub home

Nothing beyond the standard web logs above.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • We don't use tracking or advertising cookies, or analytics that identify you.
  • We don't send marketing emails.
  • Sponsors that appear on the sites don't receive any information about you.

Overseas service providers

Our service providers are based overseas (mostly in the United States) and may process or store information outside Australia:

  • Netlify: website hosting, server functions, form submissions and web logs
  • Resend: delivering SafeInstall emails
  • GoatCounter: anonymous visit counts for SafeInstall
  • Anthropic: answering Ask TechMate AI questions

We only send them what's needed for the feature you're using, and we choose providers that commit to protecting the information they handle.

Keeping information secure

The sites use HTTPS. We keep as little information as possible, and much of it never leaves your device. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't put information into the sites that you'd be seriously harmed by losing.

Access, correction and deletion

Most information is held only on your own device, and you can delete it any time. For anything we or our providers hold, such as a form submission, contact us and we'll help you access, correct or delete it where we can.

Questions and complaints

Use our contact form and choose "Privacy or legal question". Leave an email address so we can reply. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We'll update this policy if what we collect or how we use it changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
